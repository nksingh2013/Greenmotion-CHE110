01 / The challenge

Transport choices shape
the places we live.

Road transport affects energy demand, air quality, noise, public space and climate. There is no single magic fix: better outcomes come from making low-emission choices safe, reliable and practical.

6+ Gt

Road transport emissions

Global road transport emitted more than six gigatonnes of CO₂ in 2024, according to the IEA's road-transport analysis.

GLOBAL · 2024
~99%

Air-quality challenge

WHO reported that in 2019, 99% of the global population lived in places that did not meet its air-quality guideline levels.

WHO · 2019 ESTIMATE
18%

Transport energy in India

India's transport sector accounts for an estimated 18% of total energy consumption; this is an energy share, not an emissions share.

INDIA · ENERGY SHARE

Statistics describe different scopes and years; they should not be directly compared. Transport is not the only source of air pollution. See linked sources at the bottom.

05 / From idea to action

Better mobility starts
with people.

A practical campus or neighbourhood pilot should begin with people's needs, test a small change, and measure outcomes transparently.

🧭

01 · Understand the journey

  • Survey students about travel distance, cost, safety and time.
  • Map common routes, bus stops, crossings and barriers.
  • Include disabled people and those with different schedules.
🛠️

02 · Design a connected option

  • Improve walking routes to transit stops and campus gates.
  • Explore carpool matching and a voluntary shared-ride day.
  • Recommend reliable shuttles and secure cycle parking.
📍

03 · Run a small pilot

  • Set a baseline before making changes.
  • Record participation, journey time and user feedback.
  • Clearly separate measured results from estimates.
📊

04 · Measure fairly

  • Track access, affordability, safety and reliability.
  • Use consistent emission factors and explain assumptions.
  • Publish limitations and improve the plan before scaling.

One system. Many better choices.

Cleaner transport works best when it is safe, affordable and convenient for everyday people.
